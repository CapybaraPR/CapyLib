## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security and integrity of our proprietary software seriously. If you discover a security vulnerability or potential exploit, please report it **privately and responsibly**.

> **DO NOT** submit public GitHub issues, pull requests, or public discussions for security vulnerabilities or exploit details.

### How to Submit a Report

1. **GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting (Preferred)**:
   - Navigate to the repository's **Security** tab.
   - Under **Reporting**, click **Report a vulnerability** to open a confidential advisory draft.
2. **Direct Contact**:
   - Reach out directly to **CapybaraPR** via official contact channels with the subject prefix `[SECURITY]`.

### Information to Include in Your Report

To help us investigate and resolve the issue efficiently, please include:
- A clear description of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
- Step-by-step instructions or minimal proof-of-concept (PoC) to reproduce the issue.
- Affected components, methods, or endpoints.
- Any suggested remediations or workarounds, if available.

### Response Timeline

- **Acknowledgment**: Within **48 hours** of receiving your report.
- **Triage & Assessment**: Within **3–7 business days**.
- **Remediation**: Once verified, a proprietary fix will be prepared and deployed.
